Data Deletion Policy

How to request removal of your personal data from our systems.

Effective Date16 April 2025
Last Updated16 April 2025
Response TimeWithin 30 Days

Ishvanta Solutions is committed to respecting your right to privacy. This policy explains how you can request the deletion of your personal data that we hold — including data collected through our website, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and other channels.

1. Your Right to Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of any personal data we hold about you, subject to certain legal retention obligations. This right applies to:

  • Contact information submitted through our website (name, email, phone number).
  • Data collected via Facebook Lead Ads or our Facebook / Instagram pages.
  • WhatsApp communication history and phone number records.
  • Course enquiry and enrolment records (subject to mandatory retention periods).

2. How to Request Data Deletion

You can submit a data deletion request using any of the following methods:

📧 Option 1 — Email Request (Recommended)

Send an email to ishvantaacademy@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address and/or phone number associated with your records
  • A brief description of what data you would like deleted
  • Whether this is a Facebook-related data deletion request

💬 Option 2 — WhatsApp Request

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90328 00664 with the message: "I would like to request deletion of my personal data" and we will guide you through the process.

3. What Happens After You Submit a Request

  1. Acknowledgement: We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days of receiving it.
  2. Verification: We may ask you to verify your identity to ensure we delete the correct records and to prevent unauthorised deletion requests.
  3. Review: We will review your data across our systems (CRM, WhatsApp records, website enquiries, Facebook-sourced leads).
  4. Deletion: We will delete or anonymise the identified data within 30 days of verifying your request.
  5. Confirmation: We will send you a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

4. Facebook / Meta — Data Deletion Instructions

If you have interacted with Ishvanta Solutions through Facebook or Instagram (including Facebook Login, Lead Ads, or Messenger), you can request deletion of associated data as follows:

4.1 Through Facebook Settings

  • Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites.
  • Find any app or service connected to Ishvanta Solutions and remove it.
  • Additionally, email us at ishvantaacademy@gmail.com so we can delete the corresponding lead data from our records.

4.2 Direct Deletion Request (Facebook Callback)

If you used Facebook Login or submitted a lead form via Facebook, you can submit a direct data deletion request by emailing us at ishvantaacademy@gmail.com with the subject "Facebook Data Deletion Request". Please include the name and email address registered on your Facebook account.

We will confirm deletion within 30 days and provide you with a confirmation code for your records.

5. Data We May Retain Despite a Deletion Request

In certain circumstances, we are required by law to retain specific data even after a deletion request. This includes:

Data Type Retention Reason Retention Period
Financial / payment records Indian tax & accounting laws (GST) 7 years
Course completion certificates Alumni verification & legal record Duration of the institute's operation
Correspondence related to legal disputes Legal obligation Until dispute is resolved

Where data must be retained, we will anonymise all personally identifiable details to the greatest extent possible.

6. Timeframes

  • Acknowledgement: Within 3 business days.
  • Identity Verification: Within 5 business days of your response.
  • Deletion Completion: Within 30 days of verification.
  • Confirmation: Sent to your email address once complete.

7. Complaints & Escalation

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data deletion request, you may contact us at ishvantaacademy@gmail.com to escalate the matter. We are committed to resolving all concerns fairly and promptly.
