Who We Are

Ishvanta Solutions was founded in 2006 with one straightforward belief: the best way to train a tech professional is to have another tech professional teach them — face to face, with real code, real scenarios, and real feedback.

Over the past 18+ years, we've upskilled more than 3,000 mid-level IT professionals across India, helping them break into new domains, get promoted, and land better roles. Our base is in Madhapur, Hyderabad — the heart of India's tech corridor.

We are not a platform. We are not a content library. We are a tight-knit team of practitioners who show up, teach hard, and genuinely care about where our students end up.
